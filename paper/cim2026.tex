\abstract
This paper describes the compositional poetics of a fixed-media electroacoustic
work and the YAML-to-Csound system that generates it. The central claim is
architectural and aesthetic at once: a single generative variable, the rhythmic
value, governs four otherwise independent dimensions of the synthesis --- time,
pitch, space and amplitude. The composer does not write the resulting fourfold
surface; instead a source is chosen and a possibility space is declared, while a
discrete nonlinear dynamical system (a logistic map) auto-generates the rhythmic
stream. Pitch inhabits a dense 200-degree Pythagorean cloud whose beatings are
not planned degree by degree but emerge from the encounter of voices; perceptual
coherence is restored by an isophonic (ISO~226:2003) amplitude compensation. The
work is composed in deferred time: a long feedback loop of specification, offline
rendering, listening and rewriting. We argue that the graphic score emitted by
the system is itself a compositional instrument --- the third vertex of a
triangulation between parametric DSL, visual score and listening --- and we
situate the practice within a local lineage of nonlinear-iteration synthesis.
\endabstract

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:introduction}

The submitted composition is a fixed-media electroacoustic work that brings
musical \emph{states in transition} into focus. Its form is not a succession of
chosen events but the evolution of tendencies: a state that transforms
continuously from beginning to end, or that holds still. This paper is not a
technical manual of the generating system. It uses that system as instrument and
material to argue a compositional posture: \emph{control of the possibility
space, not of the detail}.

Three principles hold the work together. First, a single variable generates the
texture: the rhythmic value decides time, pitch, space and amplitude
(Section~\ref{sec:rhythm}). Second, that source is a dynamical system: the
rhythm auto-generates through nonlinear feedback, so that a seed and a regime
are fixed and the orbit emerges. Third, the frequency space produces what is not
controlled: a dense Pythagorean cloud yields beatings that arise rather than
being written. The posture is personal and situated; it is offered as a
practice, not as a prescription.

The lineage is explicit. Synthesis by iterated nonlinear functions, and the
poetics of emergence in which the composer designs the system rather than the
outcome, were formulated by Di~Scipio two decades earlier
\cite{DiScipio1990,DiScipio2001}, with the logistic map a canonical bridge from
chaos to musical parameters [Bidlack 1992, source to be retrieved]. Notably,
this is a \emph{local}
lineage: Di~Scipio and Prignano both worked at the Laboratorio Musica e
Sonologia in L'Aquila --- the host city of CIM 2026 --- on nonlinear-iteration
synthesis \cite{DiScipio1995,Prignano1995}, a line of which Rizzuti is a direct
heir \cite{Rizzuti2006}. The work inscribes itself in this tradition; it does
not found it.

\section{Rhythm as the single generative variable}\label{sec:rhythm}

In the generating system, the rhythmic value is not one parameter among many: it
is the generative parameter. A single integer per event is read by four
independent dimensions of the synthesis. Deciding the rhythm means deciding, in
the same gesture, pitch, time, space and amplitude.

\textbf{Time.} The inter-onset interval and the event duration are fractions of
a reference \emph{harmonic duration} divided by the rhythmic value: a duration
subdivided by the harmonic series, where row $i$ is split into $i$ equal parts
(Figure~\ref{fig:rhythmic}). The rhythmic value selects which subdivision sounds.

 \centerline{\includegraphics[width=\columnwidth]{\figRhythmic}}
 \caption{Rhythmic harmonics: a reference duration subdivided by the harmonic
 series. Row $i$ is split into $i$ equal parts; the rhythmic value selects which
 subdivision sounds.}
 \label{fig:rhythmic}
\end{figure}

\textbf{Pitch.} The same integer indexes the Pythagorean frequency cloud of
Section~\ref{sec:pitch}, so denser rhythms and denser pitch sampling move
together.

\textbf{Space.} The value drives a spatial harmonic distribution: lobes of
energy laid out along the circumference (Figure~\ref{fig:spatial}), so that
rhythmic articulation and stereo (mid/side) placement share a single control.

\begin{figure}
 \centerline{\includegraphics[width=\columnwidth]{\figSpatial}}
 \caption{Spatial harmonics $|\sin/\cos(i/2\cdot\theta)|^{n}$ laid out along the
 circumference: lobes of energy whose number follows the harmonic index. The
 rhythmic articulation and the stereo (mid/side) placement share one control.}
 \label{fig:spatial}
\end{figure}

\textbf{Amplitude.} The value participates in the dynamic profile, whose
physical realization is perceptually corrected (Section~\ref{sec:iso}).

To our knowledge the parameter-mapping literature covers gesture-to-synthesis or
feature-based synthesis, but not a \emph{single generative integer driving four
independent dimensions}. We offer this as the most original axis of the work,
stated as a contribution to be tested rather than a claim of priority.

\subsection{The rhythm is itself auto-generated}\label{sec:logistic}

After a short explicit initial sequence, the rhythmic stream auto-generates:
each new value is a function of the previous one, $x[n+1]=f(x[n])$, a
discrete-time dynamical system whose orbit is the music. The map is a clamped
nonlinear function with selectable regimes (a logistic family at
$r \approx 2.8$ to $3.99$), so that fixing the seed and the regime fixes a class
of orbits, not a list of values. This is the operational meaning of
``control of the possibility space'': the composer writes the seed and the
regime, the orbit emerges \cite{DiScipio1999,Rizzuti2006}.

\section{A dense Pythagorean pitch cloud}\label{sec:pitch}

The pitch space is a very dense Pythagorean cloud: not the twelve notes of the
historical Pythagorean scale but \textbf{200 intervals per octave}, generated by
the same chain of just fifths ($3/2$) reduced to the octave. Density is the
point: at 200 degrees adjacent pitches are nearly coincident, and when the
rhythm makes them sound together \emph{beatings} arise that the composer does
not control degree by degree --- they emerge from the encounter of voices.

This axis descends from Walter Branchi's work on intonation systems
[Branchi, source to be retrieved]. The extension here is quantitative and
changes its nature: the same procedure --- just fifths stacked and folded into
the octave --- pushed to a high number of degrees, not to obtain a playable
scale but to \emph{saturate} the frequency space and produce interference zones.

\subsection{Isophonic amplitude compensation}\label{sec:iso}

Dynamics in the work are \emph{perceptual}, not physical. The markings
$ppp \ldots fff$ are not dBFS levels but levels in Phon: the physical amplitude
of each event is corrected as a function of frequency by the ISO~226:2003
equal-loudness contour, so that perceived loudness stays constant across the
spectrum at a given dynamic. The isophonic amplitude is the counterweight ---
the one place where the system re-imposes a perceptual coherence on the emergent
material. The practice has methodological precedent in loudness-normalized
sonification on the 40-phon contour.

\section{The graphic score as a compositional instrument}\label{sec:score}

\begin{figure*}[t]
 \centerline{\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{\figGraphicScore}}
 \caption{The graphic score: one 60-second page of the work. Shaded envelopes
 are the declared tendency masks (the possibility space written in the YAML);
 the bars are the sampled events (the stochastic realization). Top axis: octave/
 register and harmonic duration; middle: linear dynamics; bottom: probabilistic
 dynamics. Reading the distance between declared space and sampled events is a
 compositional act.}
 \label{fig:graphicscore}
\end{figure*}

Alongside the audio, the system emits a PDF \emph{graphic score}
(Figure~\ref{fig:graphicscore}). The class that produces it is named a
``debugger'', but its function is compositional: it is the third vertex of a
triangulation between the parametric DSL, the visual score and listening. The
plot superimposes two things on a shared time axis: the declared \emph{tendency
masks} (shaded envelopes --- the possibility space written in the YAML) and the
\emph{sampled events} (the bars --- the actual stochastic realization). Reading
the distance between the declared space and what was sampled is a compositional
act: it is where the composer judges whether the orbit, not the gesture,
deserves rewriting.

This closes the loop of deferred time. The work is composed in a long feedback:
specification, offline rendering, listening, rewriting. Deferred time is a
choice, not a limitation --- separating decision from realization lets listening
become a judgement on the orbit. The non-realtime rendering of granular sound
has a historical precedent in Roads's account of compositions made ``in years''
[Roads 2001, source to be retrieved]. A language-based variant of the same
deferred cycle --- axiom, rules, rewriting, selection --- is described by
Bottoni et al. \cite{Bottoni2006}, where the musical language is itself a formal
language.

\section{The work: six embricated sections}\label{sec:work}

The concrete score unfolds in six sections of roughly 485~seconds total
(about 8'05'', within the fixed-media limit). Negative start offsets make the
sections \emph{embricate}: a section enters before the previous one ends. The
arc moves from a slow low-register birth of clusters, through a glissando solo,
a densification from clouds to swarm, a rupture that irrupts \emph{inside} the
swarm (sections III and IV overlap entirely), a vertical cluster mass, and a
final dissolving glissando. Each section is described as a field of state
tendencies, not as a sequence of events: the masks move (transition) or hold
(stasis), and the audible form is the reading of that movement.

\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

The work sits at the intersection of several lines. Deterministic chaos and the
logistic map as compositional source: Di~Scipio's functional iteration synthesis
\cite{DiScipio1990,DiScipio1999,DiScipio2001} and Bidlack's canonical mapping
[Bidlack 1992, source to be retrieved]; within the CIM proceedings, Prignano's
functional-iteration
synthesis \cite{Prignano1995}, Di~Scipio's recursive granulation
\cite{DiScipio1995}, and Rizzuti's deterministic ``sound chaos''
\cite{Rizzuti2006}. Tendency masks as directional selection within an evolving
range descend from Koenig's Project~1/2. Algorithmic growth from a single cell
is explored by Leach \cite{Leach1995}; deferred, language-based composition by
Bottoni et al. \cite{Bottoni2006}. The distinguishing move of the present work
is not the mathematics of chaos but its point of application: rhythm as the
upstream variable that propagates across four dimensions.

\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The work argues a posture through its construction: a single generative variable,
an emergent pitch space, a perceptual counterweight, and a deferred-time loop in
which the graphic score is an instrument of judgement. The form is the reading of
musical states in transition. The most original axis --- one integer driving four
independent dimensions --- is offered for scrutiny; the Branchi lineage of the
Pythagorean axis remains to be fully developed once the primary source is
secured.
